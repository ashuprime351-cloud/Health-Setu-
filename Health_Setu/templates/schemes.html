📋 Health Schemes

Government-backed healthcare schemes that may help you access affordable medical care.

ℹ️ Verification required: Eligibility rules, benefit limits, and application processes change over time. Always verify through official portals before applying.

Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY

Central Government

What it is: PM-JAY provides health cover to economically vulnerable families for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation.

  • Up to ₹5 lakh cover per family per year (verify current limit).
  • Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals nationwide.
  • Pre-existing conditions covered from day one (verify terms).

⚠ Eligibility based on SECC data and state lists. Check the official portal to confirm.

Central Govt. Health Scheme (CGHS)

Central Government

What it is: CGHS provides comprehensive healthcare to central government employees, pensioners, and dependants.

  • OPD treatment and medicines at CGHS Wellness Centres.
  • Specialist consultations at empanelled hospitals.
  • Cashless hospitalisation at empanelled private hospitals (subject to referral).

⚠ Available to eligible central govt. employees and pensioners only.

Employees’ State Insurance (ESI)

Central Government

What it is: ESI is a self-financing health insurance scheme for workers in the organised sector.

  • Full medical care for insured persons and family.
  • Cash sickness and maternity benefits.
  • Disability benefits available (verify current terms).

⚠ For employees below a notified wage threshold in covered establishments. Verify current limit.

Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

Central Government

What it is: JSY promotes institutional deliveries to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality.

  • Cash assistance to pregnant women for institutional delivery.
  • ASHA workers provide support and accompany beneficiaries.
  • Benefits vary by state (LPS vs HPS classification).

⚠ Amounts differ by state. Verify through your nearest PHC or Anganwadi centre.

National Health Mission (NHM)

Central Government

What it is: NHM (NRHM + NUHM) aims to provide accessible, affordable healthcare to rural and urban populations.

  • Strengthening public health infrastructure.
  • Free essential medicines and diagnostics at public facilities.
  • Mobile health services for remote areas.

⚠ Services vary by state. Contact your nearest government health facility for local details.
